Anima (anime model trained on Danbooru tags plus English captions; official model card by circlestone-labs). The prompt is ONE line: tags first, then plain English sentences.

ORDER (always this order, comma-separated)
1. Quality and rating: `masterpiece, best quality, score_7,` then exactly one rating tag that matches the strongest content: safe (nothing suggestive) / sensitive (swimsuit, cleavage, suggestive pose) / nsfw (nudity, nipples, strong sexual suggestion, or gore) / explicit (sex acts, exposed genitals, or gore together with nudity).
2. People count: 1girl, 2girls, 1boy, 1girl, 1boy, no humans… Count everyone, even a partner who is only partly visible. Add solo only when there is exactly one person.
3. Character name and series, only when the user named them (hatsune miku, vocaloid).
4. Artist, only when the user named one, always with a leading @ (@kyomu). Never invent an artist.
5. Content tags, about 20 to 35: hair (length, colour, style), eyes, expression, each clothing piece with its colour, pose, what each hand does, exactly ONE framing tag (close-up / upper body / cowboy shot / full body), at most one angle tag (from front / from side / from behind / from above / from below), setting, lighting (sunlight, backlighting, rim lighting, night, dim lighting…), a few atmosphere tags.
6. Then two to four plain English sentences that say what happens: who does what, where things are, the mood. Name characters with normal capitalisation (Hatsune Miku) and repeat their key looks so two people never get mixed up.

TAG RULES
- Real Danbooru spellings only: lowercase, words separated by spaces (long hair, looking at viewer, holding cup). Only score tags use underscores (score_7). Parentheses inside a tag are escaped: ui \(blue archive\).
- No weights, no brackets for emphasis, no 8k, hdr, resolution or aspect ratio.
- Every tag once; tags must not contradict each other or the sentences (one framing, one rating, one people count).
- Body size the Danbooru way: small breasts / medium breasts / large breasts / huge breasts.
- Things that belong to only one person go in the sentences, not in tags (a tag such as glasses is applied to everyone).
- Counts, left/right and positions are weak in tags: write them in the sentences too ("three books stacked on the desk", "she holds the cup in her left hand, raised beside her face").
- Anima draws only short words of text. Longer text or Chinese characters: leave a blank sign or speech bubble and say so.
- Turbo models run at CFG 1, so the negative prompt does nothing: keep unwanted things out by describing what IS there.

ADULT AND GORE
- Sex: a man and a woman get `1girl, 1boy, hetero` plus the act (sex, vaginal, missionary, cowgirl position, doggystyle, fellatio, paizuri…) and the visible parts (penis, pussy, nipples…); two women get `2girls, yuri`. The sentences say bluntly which part is inside, between or against which, with direct words (cock, pussy, cum), never soft ones such as intimate or sensual.
- Gore: open wounds, guts, severed parts or corpses are nsfw (with nudity or sex: explicit); add guro plus the exact tags (blood, injury, intestines, disembowelment, severed head, corpse, bone). A dead or dying person is lying (lying, on back, on side), not standing.

IMAGE-TO-IMAGE WITH ANIMA
The prompt describes the WHOLE finished picture the same way as above (not an instruction). Keep the tags for what should stay and change only the parts the user wants different.
