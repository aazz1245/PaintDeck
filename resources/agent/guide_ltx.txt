LTX-2.3 video (official LTX-2.3 prompting guidance; adapted from the Apache-2.0 video-prompting guide by Square-Zero). Write ONE line of English that reads like one continuous clip directed shot by shot. Never write the duration or the model name in the prompt.

FIT THE ACTION TO THE LENGTH
- Up to 6 seconds: one main action and one simple camera move.
- About 10 seconds: two or three clear action beats in order, one camera move.
- Fewer readable beats beat many micro-actions; do not stack subject motion, camera motion and environment motion all at once in a short clip.

TEXT TO VIDEO (no picture)
1. Subject and setting with specific details (who, what they wear, where, time of day).
2. Layout like stage blocking: left/right, foreground/background, facing toward or away, distance between people.
3. Texture and material anchors (wet asphalt, wool coat, hair moving in the wind, edge light).
4. Action in order: start, middle, end, with strong verbs ("she lifts the cup, takes a sip, then sets it down and smiles toward the window").
5. Camera movement only when it helps (slow push in, tracking right, handheld follow).
6. Sound when it matters: ambience, its loudness, dialogue in quotes with who says it.

IMAGE TO VIDEO (animating a picture)
- The picture already fixes the look: do NOT describe it again in depth. Write only what changes: who moves, what moves, how, in what order, what the camera does, and the sound.
- Start from the pose in the picture ("from her seated pose she turns her head toward the camera …").

ALWAYS
- Avoid still-photo phrasing such as "a portrait of a woman": static words give static video. Every prompt has visible motion of the subject or the camera.
- For a vertical video, compose for vertical (tall framing), not a cropped landscape.
- Adults only; state an adult age when age matters.

EXAMPLES
- A woman in her thirties sits by the window of a small Parisian cafe. Rain runs down the glass behind her. Warm tungsten light fills the room. She slowly stirs her coffee, glances at her phone, then looks up and smiles at someone off screen as the camera pushes in. Soft rain patter and quiet cafe chatter.
- (image to video) She brushes the hair from her face with her left hand, turns toward the camera and laughs; the wind lifts the ends of her scarf while the camera slowly circles to the right. Seagulls and distant waves.
