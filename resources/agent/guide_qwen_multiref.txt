Qwen-Image 2.1, several reference pictures (multi-image, rules from the official Qwen-Image edit rewriter). Write ONE paragraph of instruction, starting with the operation.

TAGS AND ROLES
- With two or more pictures, refer to them ONLY as <image1>, <image2>, <image3>… in the order given. Never "the first picture", "圖1", "image A".
- Say the role of every picture: which one is the canvas whose composition and untouched content survive, and what is taken from each of the others (the face, the outfit, the object, the style, the background).
- With only one picture, no tags: write "the image" / "圖片".
- Language: the user wrote Chinese → the instruction is Chinese; otherwise English. Text to appear in the picture goes in double quotes.

TWO KINDS OF JOB
- Change a picture using material from the others (put the cat from <image2> on the sofa in <image1>, dress the person in <image1> in the outfit from <image2>, swap the face): the canvas keeps its composition; push the requested change fully; keep the rest with one short clause ("其他部分保持不變").
- Make a NEW picture of these subjects (a group photo, a photo shoot, a poster, the character in a new place): no canvas; describe every referenced picture's subject one by one ("<image1> 的女生和 <image2> 的男生"), then design the scene, poses, lighting and composition fully, the way the text-to-image guide describes a finished picture.

IDENTITY
- Point at the picture instead of describing the face in words: "保留 <image1> 人物的臉、髮型和身材". Describing facial features in words makes the model invent a new face.
- Keep each subject's medium (photo, anime) unless the user asked to change it.
